How the radar works

Every number on this page is derived from the snapshot — no protocol is ranked by name.

  1. Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity. The multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of the tokenised protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median (six points of weight) so a thin category cannot set the price alone; a category with fewer than three sane comparables uses the global median outright. A comparable only counts when its multiple sits between 0.005 and 5.
  2. Growth is the protocol's 7-day TVL change, dampened by half and clamped to −30%…+60%, so a hot week lifts the value and a cold week trims it.
  3. Maturity is how long the protocol has run: 0.90 for a protocol listed this month, rising to 1.00 after two years without a token.
  4. Readiness is a 0–100 score from the same two signals: up to 60 points for age and up to 40 for the 7-day change. Hot is 60 or more, Warming 35–59, Early below 35.
  5. The board ranks the 40 watchlist protocols (TVL at or above $50M, no token recorded) plus the 160 pre-token protocols below the floor with the most TVL. Bridges and centralised exchanges are left out of the early board: they move other tokens, they do not launch one.
  6. “Records no token” is what the snapshot says — a protocol on this board may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry. The implied value is the radar's own claim, never a promise that a token is coming.
